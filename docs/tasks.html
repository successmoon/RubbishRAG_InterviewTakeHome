Task Matrix 3-Tier Architecture

🎯 Engineering Tasks & Scope

RubbishRAG is structured into three clear tiers to respect your time while allowing senior candidates to demonstrate deep AI engineering judgment.

Tier 1: Required Core

Time Budget: ~3–4 hours

Complete all 4 foundational tasks (A, B, C, D) to establish a functioning, robust baseline.

Tier 2: Choose-2 Electives

Time Budget: ~2 hours

Select any 2 of 4 deep-dive tracks (E, F, G, H) that match your expertise.

Tier 3: Staff Bonus

Time Budget: ~3–4 hours (Optional)

Design an Adaptive System-1 Decision Judge for pre-retrieval routing and dynamic top-k.

Tier 1: Required Core Tasks (All Candidates Complete)

Task A — Retrieval Characterization & Diagnostic Probing

Motivation: Never optimize a retriever blindly. You must measure baseline failure modes empirically before proposing remedies.

  • Objective: Use python3 rubbish.py bepar "<query>" and python3 rubbish.py bekesh to capture at least 10 diagnostic traces across short, long, and entity-specific queries.
  • Artifact: Document baseline Hit@3 and Top1-Top5 fused score spreads in docs/PROOF.md citing trace IDs from traces/trace.jsonl.
  • Weak Solution: Guessing failure modes without citing trace IDs or running ablations.
  • Excellent Solution: Formulating falsifiable hypotheses, plotting distributions, and isolating score anomalies with controlled probes.

Task B — Hybrid Retrieval Fusion & Length Normalization

Motivation: The server's naive linear score combination 0.5*(bm25/max) + 0.5*(dense/max) is vulnerable to outlier score distortion, while BM25 $b=0.9$ penalizes thorough, detailed answers.

  • Objective: Implement client-side rank fusion (e.g. Reciprocal Rank Fusion, $RRF = \sum \frac{1}{60 + \text{rank}}$) or length-compensated rescoring.
  • Artifact: Updated @R.retriever in rubbish_rag/pipeline.py and an ablation plot in plots/length_vs_bm25.png.
  • Weak Solution: Blindly swapping constants without explaining why rank distributions outperform raw score scales.
  • Excellent Solution: Demonstrating through comparative traces how RRF prevents outlier compression and improves recall of comprehensive gold answers.

Task C — Persian Text Normalization & Slot Extraction

Motivation: Persian credit scoring involves multi-script digits (۲۵۰ vs 250), Arabic letter variants (ي/ك/ة), and colloquial speech (میشه vs می‌شود) that cause token-level false negatives.

  • Objective: Implement canonical normalization in rubbish_rag/normalize_fa.py and domain slot parsing (banks, ranks A1..E3, persona, debt types) in rubbish_rag/slots.py.
  • Artifact: Verified unit tests in tests/test_normalization.py and tests/test_slots.py passing via pytest.
  • Weak Solution: Ad-hoc string replaces that corrupt legitimate words or alter numerical semantics.
  • Excellent Solution: Clean, idempotent transformation pipeline covering digits, half-spaces, colloquial verbs, and entity regexes with 100% test pass rate.

Task D — Chunking Strategy & Metadata Preservation

Motivation: Fixed 300-character slices sever sentences mid-phrase, drop regulatory category headers, and isolate numbers from their units.

  • Objective: Implement boundary-aware or metadata-augmented chunking in @R.chunker (e.g. prefixing [Category | Keyword] and respecting sentence delimiters).
  • Artifact: Improved chunk() function in rubbish_rag/pipeline.py.
  • Weak Solution: Increasing chunk size blindly to 1000 characters, introducing token bloat and diluted retrieval.
  • Excellent Solution: Context-preserving chunks that retain critical domain anchors while bounding segment lengths.

Tier 2: Choose-2 Elective Engineering Tracks (Select Any 2)

Choose two of the four tracks below based on your interests and strengths. You will be evaluated on the depth and empirical validation of your two chosen tracks.

Track E: Evidence Reranking & Diversity (MMR / Deduplication)

Focus: Prevent multiple chunks from the exact same document from monopolizing the top-k window.

  • Implementation: Implement document-level deduplication or Maximal Marginal Relevance (MMR) in @R.reranker to guarantee diverse evidence coverage in top-3 cites.
  • Key Metric: Document redundancy rate in top-5 and Hit@3 recall gain.

Track F: Adversarial & Noise Document Defense

Focus: Guard against keyword-stuffed fake documents injected into the index (IDs ≥ 9000).

  • Implementation: Ground candidates against verified corpus records in corpus/test.csv or implement repetition penalty scoring to demote spam. (Note: do NOT hardcode arbitrary magic numbers).
  • Key Metric: Poison citation rate in top-3 answers (must reach 0%).

Track G: Polarity & Negation-Aware Matching

Focus: Distinguish statements of opposite polarity that share high unigram overlap (e.g. "می‌شود" vs "نمی‌شود", "دارای سابقه" vs "فاقد سابقه").

  • Implementation: Implement negation-sensitive scoring that penalizes polarity inversions during candidate reranking.
  • Key Metric: Zero citations of opposing polarity chunks on negated credit regulation queries.

Track H: Grounded Resolution & Clarification Gating

Focus: Resolve queries that are intrinsically ambiguous (e.g. "بدهی دارم") rather than hallucinating an unsupported answer.

  • Implementation: In @R.resolver, return the clarification contract with dynamic multiple-choice options when key decision slots are absent.
  • Key Metric: Clarification accuracy > 70% on under-specified queries without regressing answer accuracy on specific queries.

Tier 3: Staff-Level Bonus Track (Adaptive System-1 Decision Judge)

Track I: Adaptive RAG Decision Judge

Design a fast structured decision layer in front of and within the RAG pipeline to dynamically control retrieval necessity, context budgeting, evidence sufficiency, and calibrated escalation.

  • Reference Implementation: Provided in rubbish_rag/judge.py with dataset in data/judge_dataset.json.
  • CLI Tools: python3 rubbish.py judge "<query>" and python3 rubbish.py judge-bench.
  • Full Documentation: See the dedicated Adaptive RAG Decision Judge Guide →.
