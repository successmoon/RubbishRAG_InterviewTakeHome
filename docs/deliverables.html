📋 Deliverables & Grading Standards

What you must submit, how submissions are evaluated, and the multi-dimensional assessment rubric.

Required Deliverables

1. Pipeline Implementation

Your clean overrides in rubbish_rag/ (implementing modular, production-ready logic for chunking, hybrid retrieval, fusion, reranking, and ambiguity resolution).

2. Evidence Document (PROOF.md)

A rigorous report created from docs/PROOF_template.md linking each identified failure mode and architectural decision to exact trace IDs, probe logs, and ablation plots.

3. Signed Submission Archive

Generated automatically via python3 rubbish.py bastesh containing your source code, session traces (traces/trace.jsonl), comparative plots, and SHA256 integrity manifest.

Packaging Your Submission

Run the automated packaging command from your repository root:

python3 rubbish.py bastesh

This command automatically:

  • Executes the benchmark evaluation and records your run metadata and scorecard in README.md.
  • Validates presence of your implementation, docs/PROOF.md, session traces, and generated artifacts.
  • Archives your submission into submissions/submission.zip with an immutable SHA256 manifest to guarantee reproducibility.

Multi-Dimensional Evaluation Rubric

Submissions are evaluated on senior AI engineering craft, scientific rigor, and software quality across 7 dimensions (100 points total), plus up to +20 bonus points:

Dimension Weight What We Look For
1. Problem Diagnosis & Scientific Methodology 20% Formulates falsifiable hypotheses before writing code. Designs targeted probe queries to isolate failure modes rather than guessing or blindly tweaking parameters.
2. Retrieval Precision & IR Correctness 20% Sound Persian normalization, entity-aware slot preservation, score scale calibration, length bias mitigation, and defensive filtering against irrelevant or synthetic distractors.
3. Answer Quality, Faithfulness & Guardrails 15% Accurate responses grounded strictly in retrieved context. Clean handling of ambiguity (asking structured clarifying questions) and defensive abstention when evidence is absent.
4. Software Architecture & Code Quality 15% Modular, clean, idiomatic Python code with type hints, docstrings, defensive exception handling, minimal latency overhead (<100ms per query on CPU), and zero fragile monkey-patching.
5. Observability, Telemetry & Diagnostics 10% Rich structured logging in traces/trace.jsonl with query IDs, slot extraction dumps, sub-scores, reranking deltas, latency timing, and decision branches.
6. Testing Rigor & Reproducibility 10% Deterministic execution across runs. Comprehensive unit tests covering normalization, entity extraction, reranking, and edge cases. Passes CI test suite.
7. Documentation & Engineering Communication 10% Crisp, professional docs/PROOF.md citing specific trace IDs, comparative metric tables, and ablation analysis. Clear trade-off explanations.
Bonus Tracks (Staff Track / UI / Orchestrator) +20% Implementation of the Adaptive System-1 Decision Judge (routing, dynamic $k$, confidence calibration), LangChain LCEL orchestrator, or Web Studio improvements.

Target Performance Benchmarks

Overall Accuracy

> 65% on held-out test distribution (Broken baseline: ~25–30%).

Clarification Accuracy

> 50% on ambiguous queries (Broken baseline: 0% — hallucinates ungrounded answer).

Trace Integrity

Comprehensive telemetry with ≥ 15 distinct diagnostic probes captured across testing sessions.

Technical Discussion & Code Walkthrough

During the follow-up technical interview, you will walk through your engineering decisions and methodology:

  • Walk through your investigation process and explain how your probe queries isolated specific pipeline flaws.
  • Explain the architectural trade-offs you balanced between retrieval recall, precision, latency budget, and candidate context length.
  • Demonstrate how your pipeline defends against edge cases: character normalization variations, loan vs credit card ambiguity, and adversarial distractors.
  • Present your empirical findings using the comparative plots and trace logs generated during your experiments.

Ground every claim in empirical evidence from your traces and ablation experiments.
